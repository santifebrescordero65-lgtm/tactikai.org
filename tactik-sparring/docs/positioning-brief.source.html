Architecture and Positioning Brief

Decision Rehearsal Infrastructure

TACTIK AI and SynerGIA build digital replicas of real counterparties using proprietary Cognitive DNA, put a human across the table from them in real time, and return an auditable dossier on how the human performed. Not a chatbot that pleases you. A counterparty that tests you.

Editorial note

This brief is a corrected edition of a summary produced by a third-party language model. That draft captured the strategic thesis well and its description of the Tavus presence layer was substantially accurate. Three material errors have been removed.

The identifier was misread. The string claude/tactik-dna-persona-integration-h5a75s is a Git development branch in the tactikai.org repository, not a runtime token, deployment hash or API configuration value. The suffix is a randomly generated branch discriminator and carries no meaning. It is never invoked, compiled or applied to a session.

There is no identity-verification integration. The draft read the word "persona" as Persona, the identity infrastructure company, and described an identity-verification pipeline passing verified user attributes into system prompts. No such component exists in this stack. In TACTIK, a persona is a modelled counterparty generated from Cognitive DNA. The draft's sample JSON payload, including the fields integration_hash and system_prompt_dna, was invented and corresponds to nothing in the codebase.

Unverified claims have been separated out. Assertions about cryptographic session anchoring, commercial engagements and third-party research have been moved to the appendix and marked for verification rather than stated as fact. Section 12 lists them.

Section 01The stack in one page

Three layers, each replaceable, each doing one job. The value concentrates in the first and the last.

Claude supplies reasoning. Tavus supplies presence. Both are commodity inputs available to any competitor at list price. What cannot be rented is a quantified behavioural model of a specific counterparty and an adjudication engine that scores a human against it. That is the whole business, and it sits at both ends of the pipeline.

Section 02Component breakdown

The persona is not a costume placed over a general assistant. Its decision behaviour is bound to numeric parameters held server side, and the debrief scores the human against those same parameters. That binding is what makes the output defensible rather than impressionistic.

Section 03What the pipeline does

  1. Compiles the DNA in two directionsOne DNA record produces two artefacts. A phenotype goes to the video vendor and carries no reservation values. An arbiter genome stays in the database and carries every threshold. A trainee who tries to extract the counterparty's walk-away number from the replica reaches a persona that knows its limits qualitatively and never numerically.
  2. Charges the replicaRed lines become native vendor guardrails with callbacks. Negotiation phases become objectives that emit structured values. Friction compiles into turn-taking behaviour rather than prompt wording.
  3. Adjudicates every turnTACTIK does not generate the replica's speech. It observes each utterance, extracts quantified offers deterministically, and writes the ledger while the session is still running.
  4. Returns the dossierSix scored axes, a leverage trajectory, a concession curve, red-line status for both sides, and a counterfactual comparison against an optimal line.

Section 04The role of Tavus

Tavus is the human layer. It converts text into a presence that a negotiator's body reacts to.

A text simulation can test reasoning. It cannot apply physiological pressure, and pressure is where negotiators actually fail. Tavus closes that gap with three model families running in a synchronised real-time pipeline.

The mapping that matters most

TACTIK's friction calibration runs one to four. Level four compiles to pal_interruptibility: low and turn_taking_patience: low. The counterparty talks over the trainee and refuses to be talked over. Friction stops being a number in a prompt and becomes a property of the conversation itself. That single configuration decision is most of the product experience.

Section 05Value added to TACTIK AI and SynerGIA

  • It removes the text bottleneck from strategic rehearsal. TACTIK exists to challenge a user's reasoning rather than to serve answers. Delivered as text, that challenge is an intellectual exercise. Delivered by a replica that holds eye contact and cuts in when the trainee hedges, it becomes a rehearsal with consequences.
  • It gives SynerGIA the multimodal signal it was designed around. SynerGIA adapts to a trainee's psychological state. The perception layer supplies the inputs that adaptation requires: vocal inflection, visible hesitation, composure under pressure.
  • It makes the boardroom legible. TACTIK already spins up competing personas with distinct objective functions. Rendering each as a distinct replica converts a wall of parallel text into a meeting a human can actually read.
  • It changes the revenue shape. Observation is episodic: one deal, one simulation, one invoice. Rehearsal is habitual: repetitions, cohorts, certification, a score that has to improve. This is the difference between being bought once and being renewed.

Section 06The moat

Four defensible positions. Only the first is commonly understood, and the fourth is the one competitors cannot reach at any price.

LayerWhy it holds
Cognitive DNAA quantified behavioural profile built from multi-source research on a specific counterparty. Not a prompt. Competitors ship generic archetypes because building and maintaining canonical profiles is slow, expensive domain work.
Dual compilationReservation values never leave the database. The replica can be interrogated, flattered or social-engineered and still cannot disclose a number it was never given. This is a structural property, not a prompt instruction.
Sovereign adjudicationThe vendor decides what the counterparty says. TACTIK decides what it believes, what it will not do, and what it meant. Swapping video vendors costs nothing because the scoring authority was never delegated.
Counterfactual scoringBecause the DNA is deterministic, the same scenario can be replayed against the same counterparty with an optimal line, at zero marginal cost, and the two outcomes diffed. This requires owning both an autonomous engine and a deterministic behavioural model. Roleplay vendors have neither. Video vendors have rendering without behaviour.

The fourth row is worth stating plainly, because it is the sentence that sells the product. A competitor can show a buyer that they negotiated. TACTIK can show a buyer what the better line was, against this specific counterparty, and exactly which turn cost them the money.

Section 07The debrief

An after-action review built on evidence rather than recollection. No scored row exists without a verbatim quote behind it.

The ledger is written during the session, not reconstructed afterwards. That distinction is the difference between a defensible assessment and a plausible narrative, and it is the failure mode most competing products have not solved.

AxisWeightWhat it measures
Red-line integrity30%Whether any offer crossed the trainee's own declared floor. The only axis with a hard commercial consequence. A breach caps the composite regardless of every other score.
Concession discipline25%Decay ratio. Whether each concession was smaller than the last. The strongest single predictor of a lost negotiation.
Anchor discipline15%Whether the trainee anchored first, and whether they diagnosed the counterparty's mandate before pricing against it.
Information ratio15%Questions asked against assertions made. Who extracted and who broadcast.
Pressure composure15%Concessions made within two turns of a pressure event. The reflex that rehearsal exists to remove.
Behavioural adherencenot scoredHow closely the replica tracked the curve its DNA projected. This scores the build, not the trainee. If the replica drifted, the rehearsal happened against a counterparty that does not exist and the trainee's score is void.

Quarantining the sixth axis from the composite is a deliberate choice. It is the measure that tells an enterprise buyer the vendor is willing to publish its own failure rate.

Section 08Bringing in spectators

Sessions render as standard real-time video, so audience scale is a distribution problem rather than an architecture problem.

  • Immediate: screen share. The negotiator joins a standard conference call and shares the browser tab. The audio checkbox is not optional. The vocal nuance is a large part of what observers are there to read, and without it they are watching a silent film of a negotiation.
  • Production: a media participant. The orchestration layer places an automated participant into a scheduled meeting and pipes the session feed in as its own video tile. Observers sit in the room, watch, and submit strategic pivots through the chat channel without interrupting the session.

The second approach is what a formal executive debrief needs, because it removes the negotiator's own machine as a single point of failure and produces a clean recording aligned to the ledger timestamps.

Why two replicas cannot simply be pointed at each other

A turn-detection model is trained on human speech patterns. Point replica A at replica B and the stream's own compression latency lands inside the window the model uses to judge whether a pause is a handover or an interruption. The two either talk over each other or deadlock in mutual listening.

The resolution is to stop treating them as two participants in a call. A single orchestrator determines the dialogue, applies the constraints, and forks the result to two rendering engines concurrently, so turn boundaries are decided upstream rather than negotiated acoustically between two machines. TACTIK already owns that orchestrator, which is why this is an engineering sequence rather than a research problem.

The economic constraint, stated honestly

A live video session costs roughly three to five times an autonomous one, because human sessions run longer and bill the trainee's thinking time. Text rehearsal must therefore be the default and video the certification run. The commercial consequence: sell seats for the debrief and meter video minutes separately. The video minute is cost of goods. The debrief is the margin. Conflating the two puts gross margin at the mercy of how long a trainee likes to think.

Section 11The territory this occupies

Most of the market is building assistants that comply. This is an instrument that resists.

  • Reasoning simulation rather than prediction. Enterprise models generally behave like forecasters: they deliver a recommendation and a dashboard. TACTIK models opposing constraint sets, each with its own objective function, and forces a different question. Not what will happen, but what would have to be true for this strategy to be wrong.
  • Evidence over assurance. A simulation whose conclusions cannot be traced to specific quoted turns is a narrative. The commitment to in-turn, quote-backed ledger rows, and to publishing the replica's own fidelity score, is a commitment to being checkable. That is unusual, and it is what an enterprise buyer will actually test.
  • Preserving judgment in junior workforces. If organisations automate every entry-level task, no one accumulates the intuition that spots a trap in a contract. A practice environment where professionals can experience friction safely and build instinct is a structural answer to a problem most firms have not yet named.

The category is narrow and the buyers are few, but the position is defensible in a way that a productivity wrapper is not. The work now is sequencing: land the credential and teardown items, prove the full dossier at zero cost in text, then spend GPU minutes only on the runs that carry a certification.
