How it works

1
Paste your material
Any conversation, negotiation transcript, email thread, meeting notes, or document. Spanish or English. Any format.
2
The engine analyzes
TACTIK's 9-stage proprietary pipeline extracts cognitive patterns, evaluates decision quality, and identifies every actor's real incentive structure.
3
You get the READOUT
Decision Intelligence score (with formula), Cognitive DNA of every actor, risks identified, next actions, and the challenge coming that you haven't seen yet.
4
A permanent URL is generated
Every debrief becomes a shareable, archivable artifact. Send it to your team. Load it into the next context. It doesn't evaporate.

What every READOUT contains

🎯
Decision Intelligence %
Weighted score across 5 dimensions with bands. Not a magic number — an auditable formula.
🧬
Cognitive DNA
Fear triggers, leverage points, deal breakers, optimal approach. Operational before a meeting.
📊
PCG Scores
F1–F5 performance grid. Initiative, depth, advance, coherence, independence.
⚠️
Risks Identified
Specific risks tagged MEDIUM / LOW / HIGH. Grounded in your actual content.
🔭
Your Next Challenge
The stakeholder who will pressure-test your position next. Named and profiled before they arrive.
🔗
Permanent URL
Every debrief lives at a public link. Share it, archive it, load it into any future session.
